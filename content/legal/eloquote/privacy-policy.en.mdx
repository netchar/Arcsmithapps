## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ArcSmith Apps ("Eloquote," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the Eloquote app for Android and iOS, including quote browsing, quote creation, saved collections, home-screen widgets, daily notifications, and premium subscription features.

Most of this policy applies to both platforms. Where the Android app and the iOS app behave differently, the text says so. Some features are available on one platform only or arrive there later. Home-screen widgets are currently available on Android only.

## Local-First Design

Eloquote is designed as a local-first app. Your quotes, saved collections, category preferences, widget configurations, and notification settings are stored on your device in a local database and local settings files. We do not operate user accounts, cloud sync, or a remote server that stores your personal content.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored locally. It is not sent to us, except for the summary values listed under "Analytics and Crash Reporting":

- Quotes you create, edit, or save to your collection
- Your selected quote categories and interests (12 categories: Motivation, Life, Love, Wisdom, Mindfulness, Success, Happiness, Friendship, Creativity, Self Improvement, Humor, and Philosophy)
- Widget configurations — template, colors, typography, refresh schedule, and content source (Android)
- Notification preferences — schedule mode, time slots, quiet hours, and content source
- App theme (light, dark, or system default), app language, and quote appearance settings such as background and text size
- A photo you choose as a quote background. The app keeps a copy of that one photo in its own storage. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library.
- Reading streak data — the days on which you read quotes
- Discovery history and browsing state

Device backups can include this data:

- **Android:** Android Auto Backup can save it to your Google account if device backup is enabled in your Android settings. That backup is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.
- **iOS:** iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer can include it if you use these features. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

## Information Processed by Third-Party Services

When you purchase or restore Eloquote Pro (a subscription or a Lifetime purchase), the following information is processed by our subscription infrastructure provider (RevenueCat) and by the store that sold it — Google Play on Android, or the Apple App Store on iOS:

- Subscription status, plan type, purchase token or transaction receipt, and renewal state
- An anonymous app user identifier generated by RevenueCat (not linked to your name or email)
- Device and app metadata such as app version, OS version, device model, and the country/region of your store account

We do not collect or store your name, email address, payment card details, or the credentials of your Google or Apple account. All payment processing is handled by Google Play or Apple.

**Refund requests on iOS.** When you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Eloquote, Apple asks us for information about that purchase. RevenueCat answers on our behalf within Apple's deadline. The answer can include whether the purchase was delivered, whether a free trial came before it, how much of the purchase was used, the purchase and refund history of the anonymous app user identifier, and our preference on the refund. It does not include your name, email address, or the content of your quotes. Apple uses this information to decide on the refund and handles it under its own privacy policy. You agree to this sharing when you purchase Eloquote Pro on iOS (see our Terms of Service). If you do not want us to share this information, write to us at the address below before you request the refund, and include your Apple order ID.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

In release builds, we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (both Google services) to understand how Eloquote is used and to diagnose failures:

- **Firebase Analytics** records app interaction events such as which screens you open, when you save or share a quote, widget and notification setting changes, and paywall actions. An event can include the category and an internal identifier of a quote, but never its text. Alongside those events, up to seven per-install properties describe the installation: your premium status ("pro" / "free"), the selected theme mode, the selected app language, the number of widgets on your home screen, whether at least one widget is placed, the range of your reading streak, and whether the reading streak is turned on. Firebase also records general device information such as device model, OS version, app version, and approximate region.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** records crash reports, application-not-responding events, and short breadcrumb logs describing what happened before a crash. The reports include device model, OS version, app version, and diagnostic values such as the name of the most recent event, the name of the most recently viewed screen, and the local database schema version.

Identifiers used by these services:

- Analytics data is linked to a Firebase-generated app instance identifier. It resets if you clear app data or reinstall the app.
- **Android:** Firebase Analytics also collects the Advertising ID of your device, unless you have deleted it in your Android settings.
- **iOS:** the app does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for permission to track you.

We do not attach your name, email address, quote text, search terms, your background photo, or any account identifier to these events. Both services are disabled in debug builds used during development.

## Advertising Measurement

We advertise Eloquote through Google Ads. Our Firebase Analytics data is linked to our Google Ads account. Google uses this data to report which of our ad campaigns led to installs and in-app actions, and it may use the data to decide who sees our ads. On Android, this data includes the Advertising ID.

Eloquote itself shows no ads and integrates no advertising SDKs.

To stop the use of your Advertising ID on Android, open your device settings, find the ads settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and choose "Delete advertising ID". You can also reset it there.

## App Rating

Eloquote may show the rating dialog provided by Google Play or the App Store. The store handles your rating and review under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

**Android.** Eloquote uses the following permissions:

- **Internet** — required for subscription verification, analytics, and crash reporting
- **Notifications** (Android 13+) — required to deliver daily quote notifications; you can disable this at any time in your device settings
- **Technical permissions added by the libraries we use** — network state, run at startup, wake lock, and foreground service (they keep widget updates and notification schedules working in the background and after a restart), Google Play billing, and Advertising ID and install referrer (Firebase Analytics)

**iOS.** Eloquote asks for the following permissions:

- **Notifications** — asked when you turn on daily quote notifications; you can disable this at any time in your device settings
- **Add to Photos** — asked only when you choose to save a quote image to your photo library from the share sheet

On both platforms, you choose a background photo in the system photo picker. The picker needs no permission, and the app receives only the photo you choose.

## How We Use Information

- Verify your subscription status and deliver premium features
- Process subscription purchases and restorations through Google Play, the Apple App Store, and RevenueCat
- Answer refund requests from Apple with information about the purchase, through RevenueCat
- Understand how Eloquote is used in aggregate and diagnose crashes, through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics in release builds
- Measure the results of our advertising campaigns through Google Ads
- Provide daily quote notifications and home-screen widgets based on your local preferences
- Comply with legal obligations where applicable

## Sharing Quotes

Eloquote allows you to share quotes to other apps as text or as an image, using the standard share sheet of your device. The image is created on your device. When you share a quote, the content is sent directly to the app you choose. We do not receive or store the content you share. Analytics records that a share happened, its format (text or image), and the category and internal identifier of the quote. It does not record the quote text or the app you chose.

## Legal Bases for EEA and UK Users

If you are in the EEA, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** — when we provide the app, subscription features, and requested functionality
- **Legitimate interests** — for subscription fraud prevention, including answers to refund requests from Apple, service integrity, understanding app usage, diagnosing crashes, and measuring the results of our advertising
- **Consent** — for delivering notifications when you grant notification permission
- **Compliance with legal obligations** — such as tax and consumer protection duties

You can object to processing that relies on legitimate interests. To do so, contact us at the address below.

## International Data Transfers

Google and RevenueCat can process data in the United States and in other countries outside your own. They apply their own safeguards for such transfers, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Data Retention

Your locally stored data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Subscription records held by RevenueCat, Google Play, and Apple are retained according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Firebase Analytics keeps user-level and event-level data for a limited period set in Google Analytics, at most 14 months.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data in Eloquote:

- Delete any saved or user-created quotes directly in the app
- Change your category interests, widget settings, notification preferences, and background photo at any time
- Disable notifications through your device settings or within the app
- Remove widgets from your home screen at any time
- Clear all app data (Android) or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored information
- Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Android) or in your Apple Account settings (iOS)
- Delete or reset your Advertising ID in your Android settings
- Turn off device backup for Eloquote in your Android or iCloud settings

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants additional privacy rights (such as access, portability, objection, or erasure requests related to data held by our service providers), contact us at support.eloquote@arcsmithapps.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

## Children

Eloquote is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address. Premium subscription purchases require a Google Play account or an Apple Account, which are subject to the age requirements of Google and Apple.

## Security

Your data is stored locally in the private storage of the app, which is protected by the operating system of your device. Subscription verification, analytics, and crash reporting use secure HTTPS connections. No service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice in the app or by other appropriate means. The updated version becomes effective on the stated "Last updated" date.
